Casa NieblaHillside house, 410 m²Concept, 2026

We start with the ground,not the drawing.

Before a single line, we walk the site at three times of day and dig two test pits. The slope, the water and the evening light decide where the house sits. Then we stay through the build, so what was drawn is what gets poured.

  • 38houses finished
  • 11years on hillsides
  • 1team, from survey to keys

Four materials,nothing applied.

Every surface is the material itself, left to weather. No paint to renew, no cladding that pretends.

  1. 01

    Board-formed concrete

    The grain of the formwork boards and the form ties, kept as poured.

  2. 02

    Charred larch

    Shou sugi ban: burned, brushed and oiled. Fifty years without a coat.

  3. 03

    Oak soffit

    The ceiling runs out through the glass and becomes the eave.

  4. 04

    Limestone and water

    Honed slabs, a basalt edge and a pool lit from its walls.

How a house gets made

  1. 01

    Site walk

    1 day

    Morning, noon and dusk on your land. Test pits, sun paths, the neighbours’ views.

  2. 02

    Sketch

    3 weeks

    Two options drawn by hand and a physical model you can hold.

  3. 03

    Drawings and permits

    3–4 months

    Structure, energy and every detail drawn before the first quote.

  4. 04

    Build

    10–14 months

    Our site architect on the ground every week; one fixed contract.

  5. 05

    Handover

    1 evening

    We turn the lights on with you at blue hour, and come back after the first winter.
